NEO·EYE

NEAR-EARTH OBJECTS · EARLY-WARNING, YIELD & EXPOSURE
What it does: early-warning (what is coming close, and when), yield (how much energy an object would release if it hit) and exposure (how close Earth and its satellites come to it).

What am I looking at?

  1. Earth is in the middle. The Sun, Moon and planets are where they really are right now.
  2. Coloured dots far out are the ~43,000 known near-Earth asteroids and comets, each moving along its real orbit.
  3. Orange lines are real asteroid flybys past Earth this month, straight from NASA JPL.
  4. Close to Earth are satellites (cyan) and debris (red). Zoom into Earth to see their names, icons and trails.
  5. Right-hand list: upcoming close approaches. Click one to fly there. 1 LD = the Moon’s distance (384,400 km).
  6. Bottom bar: run time faster or backwards, and change the lens. Hover over any button for an explanation.

Controls

Drag to rotate · scroll or pinch to zoom · click an object for details · Esc to clear · ▣ Panels for a clean view.
Data from NASA/JPL (SBDB, CNEOS CAD & Sentry, Horizons) and CelesTrak, refreshed every 6 hours. Violet “modelled” dots are statistical, not real objects.
